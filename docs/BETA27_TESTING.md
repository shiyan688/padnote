# 笔记移除与恢复 / Removing and restoring notes

## 找回已移除的笔记

在书架打开 **已移除笔记**，选择要找回的笔记，检查材料摘要，再确认恢复。支持恢复的笔记会以原来的身份回到书架，不会另建一份重复副本。

恢复包含该版本已保存的文字、手写、PDF 背景、封面、视频和知识库材料。确认前会列出材料摘要。编辑草稿与 AI 会话另行保存，不属于这份完整笔记版本的材料清单。

如果笔记在检查后发生变化，旧确认会失效。重新打开列表并检查当前内容，再决定是否恢复。恢复不会自动应用旧电脑任务的结果。

列表只包含采用完整版本存储、且保留了移除历史的笔记。这项恢复入口需要 Android 8.1 或更高版本。旧格式笔记的永久删除，以及在更早版本中已永久删除的笔记，不能通过此入口找回；删除确认中的提示会区分两种情况。

## 删除或恢复未顺利完成时

- **删除未提交：**原笔记及材料保留。先查看界面提示，再决定是否重试。
- **删除结果待确认：**先重新打开书架，检查同一笔记的状态。应用会保留材料，等待确认删除记录；不要手工删除应用目录中的文件。
- **已删除，清理待完成：**删除已经生效，笔记会从书架移除。残留材料会保留以供后续处理，不能将这条提示理解为笔记仍未删除。
- **恢复结果无法确认：**应用会刷新书架。先检查书架及 **已移除笔记** 中同一笔记的状态，再决定是否重试；不要为了恢复而再次发送原来的电脑任务。

文字编辑草稿的检查与丢弃是独立功能，见[草稿恢复指南](BETA26_TESTING.md)。分享笔记见[PDF 指南](BETA25_TESTING.md)。

## Restoring a removed note

Open **Removed notes** on the bookshelf, select a note, review its material summary, and confirm restoration. A supported note returns with its original identity rather than becoming a duplicate copy.

Restoration includes the selected version's saved text, handwriting, PDF background, cover, videos, and knowledge-base materials. Editing drafts and AI conversations are stored separately and are not members of that complete note version.

If the note changes after inspection, the old confirmation expires. Reopen the list to inspect the current version. Restoring a note does not automatically apply results from old computer tasks.

This list contains notes with complete version storage and retained removal history. The recovery entry requires Android 8.1 or later. It cannot recover permanent deletions of legacy-format notes or notes permanently deleted by earlier versions. The removal confirmation distinguishes retained-history notes from permanent deletion.

## When an operation does not finish normally

- **Deletion did not commit:** the original note and its materials remain. Read the displayed result before retrying.
- **Deletion outcome needs verification:** reopen the bookshelf and check the same note. Materials stay intact while the removal record is verified. Do not manually delete app-private files.
- **Deleted, cleanup pending:** the deletion took effect and the note leaves the shelf. Remaining materials are retained for later handling; this does not mean deletion is still pending.
- **Recovery result could not be confirmed:** the app refreshes the bookshelf. Check the same note on the bookshelf and in **Removed notes** before deciding whether to retry. Do not resubmit the original computer task as a recovery step.

Editing-draft inspection is a separate feature; see the [draft recovery guide](BETA26_TESTING.md). For sharing, see the [PDF guide](BETA25_TESTING.md).

Downloads and feedback are available from the [PadNote repository](https://github.com/shiyan688/padnote). Include the device, steps and displayed message when reporting a problem; keep private notes and credentials out of the report.
